# Vendor Onboarding Notes (loosely governed - team wiki, last edited 2024)

NOTE: this page is maintained ad hoc by the procurement team and is known to
lag the official policy. Treat figures here as indicative only.

Vendor onboarding usually takes about two weeks. Purchase orders under
Rs 50,000 could historically be approved by a team lead. Security review was
optional for vendors who do not touch customer data.
