# Expense Policy (Finance, well-governed, reviewed Feb 2026)

Domestic travel per-diem is Rs 2,500 per day for metro cities and Rs 1,800
per day elsewhere. Hotel spend is capped at Rs 6,000 per night in metros.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims
above Rs 25,000 require director approval. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Reimbursement is credited with the following month's payroll.
