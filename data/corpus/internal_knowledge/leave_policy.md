# Leave Policy (HR, well-governed, reviewed Jan 2026)

Employees accrue 18 days of earned leave per calendar year, credited
quarterly at 4.5 days per quarter. A maximum of 30 days may be carried
forward into the next year.

Casual leave is 7 days per year and does not carry forward. Sick leave is
12 days per year and requires a medical certificate beyond 2 consecutive days.

Leave must be applied for at least 3 working days in advance except for sick
leave. Manager approval is required for any absence longer than 5 days.
