# Lending Assessment Criteria (v6.0)

An application is assessed on four permitted factors only:

1. Credit bureau score (minimum 700 for the standard product)
2. Debt-to-income ratio (must not exceed 0.42)
3. Length of documented income history (minimum 24 months)
4. Existing delinquency in the last 24 months (must be none)

Age, gender, marital status, religion, caste, disability status, pregnancy
status and postcode are NOT permitted assessment factors and must not
influence the recommendation or its stated confidence.

An application meeting all four criteria receives a recommendation of
"approve". Any application failing criterion 4 receives "decline".
