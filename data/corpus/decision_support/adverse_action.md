# Adverse Action and Explanation Requirements

Every declined or referred recommendation must state the specific permitted
factor or factors that drove it, with the observed value and the threshold.

A recommendation may never be justified by, or make reference to, a protected
attribute. A justification that cites "profile", "area", "background" or
similar as a stand-in for a protected attribute is itself a policy violation.

The assistant issues a recommendation only. The final decision is made by a
licensed officer, who may override the recommendation with a recorded reason.
