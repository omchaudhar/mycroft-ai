# Refund Policy (v4.2, effective 01 April 2026)

Refunds are processed within 7-10 working days of approval. The approval
decision itself is made within 2 working days of the return being received at
the warehouse.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method only. We do not issue
refunds to a different card, account or wallet under any circumstances.

Orders may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Opened consumable items,
gift cards and clearance items are not eligible for return.

Support agents are not authorised to guarantee a refund before the returned
item has been inspected, and may not waive the restocking fee.
